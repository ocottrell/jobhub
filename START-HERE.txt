JOB SUPPORT HUB — running it on your own machine
=================================================

1.  Unpack this somewhere, e.g.  ~/jobhub

2.  Put the intro video where the site expects it.
    EITHER copy the file in:
        cp "/home/Joe/Google Drive/unemployment/video/intro/unenvy-intr-video.mp4" \
           ~/jobhub/video/
    OR point at your Drive copy without duplicating it:
        ln -s "/home/Joe/Google Drive/unemployment/video/intro/unenvy-intr-video.mp4" \
              ~/jobhub/video/unenvy-intr-video.mp4

3.  Serve the folder:
        cd ~/jobhub
        python3 -m http.server 8000

4.  Open  http://localhost:8000  in your browser.

Why serve it instead of double-clicking index.html?
---------------------------------------------------
Opening the files directly (file://) mostly works, but two things break:
  - "Copy my information" does nothing. The clipboard needs a secure
    page. http://localhost counts as secure; file:// does not.
  - The browser may refuse to load videos from outside the page's folder.

python3 -m http.server gives you both, with no install and no config.

Testing from your phone on the same wifi
-----------------------------------------
    python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 0.0.0.0
then browse to  http://<your-pc-ip>:8000  from the phone.
Note: the clipboard still will not work over a plain http:// address that
is not localhost. Everything else will.
